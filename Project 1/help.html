FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Travel Questions

How do I track my train directly from my bookings without typing PNR each time?

Simply navigate to "My trips" in the top navigation. On your booked ticket card, click "Track this train". The train tracker will automatically load your journey, current GPS location, upcoming station halts, and platform change alerts without requiring any manual entry!

How does Free Cancellation Guarantee work?

When you opt for Free Cancellation Guarantee during booking (₹149/passenger), you receive a 100% full refund of your ticket fare if cancelled up to 4 hours prior to chart preparation, with zero railway clerkage deductions.

When is the final reservation chart prepared?

As per Indian Railways guidelines, the first reservation chart is typically prepared 4 hours prior to scheduled train departure from the originating station. For early morning trains (departing before 06:00 AM), the chart is prepared at 08:00 PM the previous evening.

What should I do if my payment was deducted but ticket wasn't generated?

In rare banking timeout events, transactions are automatically reconciled by IRCTC and banking gateways within 2 hours. If a PNR was not allotted, your full amount is automatically refunded to your original payment method within 24–48 hours.
